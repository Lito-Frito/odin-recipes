GULAI SAYUR (Veggie CURRY)

Description

Gulai is an easy to make vegetable curry that is both flavorful and nutritious.

Ingredients

Gulai:

  • 1 Stalk of Lemongrass
  • 1 Bay Leaf
  • 2 Kaffir Lime Leaves
  • 1 Piece of Crushed Galangal (Ginger works as a substitute but use 3x the amount)
  • 2 Bunches of Kale (can also substitute with collard greens or other leafy greens)
  • Salt
  • Sugar
  • White Pepper
  • 14 Oz Can of Unsweetened Coconut Milk

Spice Paste:

  • 4 Tbsp Water
  • 4 Shallots
  • 2 Cloves of Garlic
  • 3 Red Chilies
  • 2 Candlenuts
  • 1/4 Tsp Coriander
  • 1/4 Tsp Black Pepper
  • 1/4 Tsp Turmeric

Directions

Steps:

  1. Bring water to a boil and then add 1 tsp of salt.
  2. Once boiling, cut kale or greens. Boil until half cooked.
  3. Remove and drain the greens.
  4. Saute the ground spices to make their fragrance "bloom".
  5. Add the coconut milk and stir until it comes to a boil.
  6. Add the greens, salt, sugar, and white peppers.
  7. Simmer for 10 minutes, adjusting seasonings as needed. Cook until desired texture for greens (e.g. crunchy or soft) is reached.
